BREAKING NEWS

Olivet Highschool Band has faced many challenges over the 26-27 school year already.

With just a week of school and only a month of band under their belts the students have already faced 3 differnt directors and 3 differnt marching styles.
Director Mary Lynn Edwards resigned a week before band camp with seemingly no notice to anyone. Luckly the old director from 10 years prior, Dave Funk, was willing to step in and help the students through band camp.
Band camp went intersting, with a new marching style, teaching styles and overall more intense approach to the marching, the students struggled through. After band camp Dave Funk went back into retirment
leaving them in the hands of a very capable director Guerry Love.

Speaking to Guerry

Now blancing life, a new family, and a new job, Guerry has his plate full. Speaking to Guerry has given us insight on what he wants this band to be, Guerry said, This band is whatever the students want it to be.
I asked at the begining of my time here at Olivet if they would like to be a competitive marching band or not, they said they would so that's what I intend to make them into. So far they have gone thiugh 3 differnt
directors in a very short time span, given that, they are doing a excelent job at learning the music and drill. By the first football game we hope to play 3 out of 4 of the peices. Overall I think this will be a great year. Thank you.

Speaking to The Students

Some of the students mentions Guerry's new baby, they said he hasn't been sleeping much, which is to be expected being a new parent, but they said hes been doing a great job being a teacher regardless.
The students have also relayed how difficult it was to go from one director to another to another, but that it might have been for the best, they all aggree that Guerry is a excelent teacher and director and they are excited to see wha this year turns out to be like
