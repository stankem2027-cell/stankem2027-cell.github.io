How to Embed a Video

A beginner's guide to adding videos to your website

Step 1: Find a Video

First, open YouTube and find a video you want to put on your website.

Step 2: Click Share

Underneath the video, click the Share button to open the sharing options.

Step 3: Select Embed

Click the Embed option. This will show you the HTML code needed for your website.

Step 4: Copy the Code

Copy the iframe code that YouTube provides.

Step 5: Add It to Your Website

Open your HTML file and paste the iframe code where you want the video to appear.
