Meet the Hobbits

Meet the four hobbits who traveled across Middle-earth on an adventure to save the world from darkness.

Frodo Baggins

Frodo is the main hobbit of the adventure. He inherits the One Ring from his uncle Bilbo and accepts the responsibility of carrying it to Mordor. He is brave, determined, and willing to sacrifice everything to protect Middle-earth.

Samwise Gamgee

Sam is Frodo's best friend and most loyal companion. He travels with Frodo throughout the journey and never gives up on him. Sam is hardworking, brave, and always willing to help his friends.

Meriadoc (Merry) Brandybuck

Merry is one of Frodo's closest friends. He joins the adventure to help protect Middle-earth. He is clever, curious, and courageous. Merry eventually becomes involved in the defense of Rohan.

Peregrin (Pippin) Took

Pippin is Merry's close friend and fellow adventurer. He is energetic, funny, and sometimes gets himself into trouble. Despite making mistakes, Pippin proves himself to be a brave member of the Fellowship.
